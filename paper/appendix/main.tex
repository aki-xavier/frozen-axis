how it is reproduced, what its axiom budget is, which paper item each machine-checked declaration discharges, and under
exactly which premises the load-bearing declarations hold. The paper's verification section (Section~\pSecVerify) states
the claims; this appendix makes them inspectable without access to the artifact.
